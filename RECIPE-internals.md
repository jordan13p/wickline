# Wickline — internals

Build details behind [RECIPE.md](RECIPE.md)'s Built from, needed only when rebuilding Wickline: each request and the fields it reads, the stored layouts, per-module notes beyond the module map, the logos and the icon drawing, and each source's detailed rules (Yahoo TW Stock's acceptance, clock, line and TW-only rules, two MIS field notes, Yahoo's exchange codes). Hosts, programs and storage locations are named in RECIPE.md; nothing here changes them.

## Network requests

Each source's requests and the fields its decoder reads (headers, hosts and the person's URLs under RECIPE.md's Network):

- `https://query1.finance.yahoo.com/v8/finance/chart/<symbol>?interval=5m&range=1d&includePrePost=false`: `chart.result[0].meta` (`regularMarketPrice`, `regularMarketTime`, `exchangeName`, `previousClose` else `chartPreviousClose`, `currency`, `longName`/`shortName`, `regularMarketDayHigh`/`Low`, `currentTradingPeriod.pre/regular/post`) and `indicators.quote[0]` (`close` for the line, the first `open`). A response whose `meta.symbol` differs from the request is rejected.
- `https://data-api.binance.vision/api/v3/ticker/24hr?symbols=["BTCUSDT",...]`: `lastPrice`, `prevClosePrice`, `openPrice`, `highPrice`, `lowPrice`, `closeTime`.
- `https://data-api.binance.vision/api/v3/klines?symbol=BTCUSDT&interval=15m&limit=96`: closes (index 4) for the 24-hour line.
- Finnhub (chosen, key saved): `https://finnhub.io/api/v1/quote?symbol=AAPL` (or the saved Finnhub URL with `/quote?symbol=`), header `X-Finnhub-Token: <key>`: `c`, `pc`, `o`, `h`, `l`, `t`.
- Yahoo 奇摩股市 (on, consented): `https://tw.stock.yahoo.com/_td-stock/api/resource/StockServices.stockList;symbols=2330.TW,6488.TWO`: rows matched on `symbol`: `symbolName`, `regularMarketTime` (ISO), `exchangeDataDelayedBy`, `exchange`, `currency`, and as `{ raw }` (`-` when none) `price`, `regularMarketPreviousClose`, `regularMarketOpen`, `regularMarketDayHigh`/`Low`, `limitUpPrice`/`limitDownPrice`. Lines: `…/FinanceChartService.ApacLibraCharts;type=tick;symbols=%5B%222330.TW%22%5D`: `[0].chart` with `meta.symbol`, `meta.currentTradingPeriod`, for a future `meta.sourceTradingPeriods` (its sessions, nested by day; kept flat and sorted as `periods.segments`), `timestamp` (s) and `indicators.quote[0].close`. The symbol's kind (`kindOf`) picks the feed clock and the day key (`tradingDay`): a future's (`WTX&`, rows from TFE only) or a stock's (stocks and indices, TAI and TWO together).
- MIS (on, consented): `https://mis.twse.com.tw/stock/api/getStockInfo.jsp?ex_ch=tse_2330.tw|otc_6488.tw&json=1&delay=0` (or the saved MIS URL with the same query): `msgArray[]` rows matched on `ex` and `c`, with `z` (last trade, `-` when none), `b`/`a` (best bids and asks, `_`-separated), `y`, `o`, `h`, `l`, `n`, `tlong` (ms), `u`/`w` (limit up and down).
- TWSE holiday schedule (while the list holds a Taiwan symbol, once per Taipei date): `https://openapi.twse.com.tw/v1/holidaySchedule/holidaySchedule`, a JSON array whose rows give `Name` (the closure's reason, kept as given) and `Date` (ROC, `1151225`: year 115 + 1911, month, day); `Weekday` and `Description` are not read. A row whose `Name` ends in 交易日 is a notice of a trading day (國曆新年開始交易日), not a closure; a Saturday or Sunday date is dropped; any other name is a closed weekday (市場無交易，僅辦理結算交割作業 included). Only the first fetch of a Taipei date holds a Taiwan round, by the 10 s timeout at most; a failure is retried 1 h after it started, beside the rounds, and never reaches the status line. Kept in memory only. A listed date reads `holiday` for stocks and indices from 00:00 Taipei; for futures from 05:00 when the weekday before ran a night session into it, else from 00:00. From 00:00 to 05:00 after a listed weekday, the night it removed reads closed (`日盤 08:45–13:45 · 夜盤休市`). A trade at or after the current session's open in any row of a market (stocks and indices: 09:00; futures: 08:45, 15:00, or the day before's 15:00 until 05:00) overrides both for every row of that market. Licence: Open Government Data License, version 1.0; its attribution statement: 「臺灣證券交易所 有價證券集中交易市場開（休）市日期（每年最新版）；此開放資料依政府資料開放授權條款 (Open Government Data License) 進行公眾釋出，使用者於遵守本條款各項規定之前提下，得利用之。https://data.gov.tw/license」
- Inferred closures (`No trades today`, `今日無成交`; never 休市, which only the schedule gives): per Taiwan market and session (stocks and indices: 09:00; futures: 08:45, and 15:00 until 05:00), a copy keeps in memory whether an answer carried a trade at or after the open, the newest trade any answer carried (whatever its day), and each source's latest answer: when it arrived and whether the source is real time. Yahoo 奇摩股市's stock list reports `newestTrade` per market clock: the newest `regularMarketTime` among the requested rows with a positive price, a trade time and `exchangeDataDelayedBy` 0 (futures only from TFE), so a delayed or `-` row is neither an answer nor a trade; MIS gives each quote's `tlong`, Yahoo Finance each Taiwan quote's time (real time by its exchange). Only sources that are on count, listed real time first, each kind in `PROVIDERS` order (Yahoo 奇摩股市, MIS, Yahoo Finance): turning one off drops its reading and note at once. A session reads no trades from its open + 10 min with a real-time answer, or + 30 min with a Yahoo Finance one (20 min late), each only when that kind's latest answer arrived at or after the deadline − 2 min, so one answer at the open and then an outage never counts; the first counting answer names the source on the status line, so the note never alternates. The deadlines are row edges (`deadlineOf`, carried as `noTradesDeadline`): a redraw, not a round. Veto: a futures trade since 08:45 blocks a stock inference; a stock trade since 09:00 blocks the futures day session's. The night: after a day that traded (its day session, the night answers' newest trade at or after 08:45, or stocks since 09:00), the closure `nightNoTrades`: `已收盤 · 13:45`, `日盤 08:45–13:45 · 夜盤尚無成交`, and `<source> 夜盤尚無成交。`; after a day without trades, `今日無成交` again. On such a day the day session's reading carries from 15:00 to the night's deadline, and from 00:00 to 05:00 the night's reading of the day before holds (`今日無成交` until 05:00), unless the new date is listed (休市). A listed day comes first; a trade since the open clears everything at once; a weekend never reads it.

Yahoo 奇摩股市, past the request above: A future's row counts only while at most 5 min ahead of the local clock, on any day (so for today from a later day too: a clock a little behind Yahoo's at 15:00); a future's trading day starts at 15:00 in Taipei and is the next weekday's (Friday night is Monday's), holidays unknown. Every row priced today is timed at the newest of today's trades on its own market clock: futures (TFE) on theirs, stocks and indices (TAI and TWO together, whatever `exchange` says) on the stock clock, so a quiet stock never takes a night futures trade (`tradeTime` keeps its own). Its line (the trading day's one-minute closes, a future's from 15:00 the day before, so the night's minutes stay past midnight; a quiet minute carrying the last close, leading empty minutes and any after now (a future's answer lists the whole day) dropped; `meta.symbol` must match) replaces Yahoo's only while its price is shown and of the line's day, ending at that price. A failed, empty or frozen line (on arrival, its last minute with a close over 2 min before the newest trade seen at this or an earlier arrival whose minute ended over 2 min ago, as a line holds a trade only once its minute ends, that trade capped at the end of the line's regular session (the answer's `currentTradingPeriod`, else the symbol's known periods), so a stock's 14:30 fixed-price trade never counts; so with lines 2 min apart a frozen line goes 4 min after its first arrival, 485-595 s after it stops mid-session, at once if first asked after the close over 3 min after the last trade, and stays gone, also through a dropped price), or losing the price, drops the line; a source failure also shows on the status line until that symbol's next line arrives. A `^TW…` index Yahoo Finance lacks is priced here alone while on (TW-only, in memory): market from its `exchange`, session from its line's `currentTradingPeriod`, an earlier day's row as its closed quote at its own time, not found until priced or named. Once priced, a TW-only row keeps its last price through a failed round or a rejected or missing row (shown cached, with the reason) and through a name-only answer (unmarked).

TWSE MIS, past the request above: Codes keep case (`tse_00632R.tw`; lower case gets an empty answer). `provider` (0.3.1's key) is written from `mis` on every save and wins on load (a downgrade keeps the choice).

## Stored layouts

The storage RECIPE.md lists, field by field:

- `shared("wickline.watchlist.v1")`: `{ version: 1, symbols: [...], paused }`.
- `shared("wickline.settings.v1")`: `{ version: 1, language: "en"|"zh-TW"|"zh-CN"|"ja"|"ko", colors: "auto"|"red-up"|"green-up", languagePromptDismissed, keyRev, sources: { us: { provider: "yahoo"|"finnhub", finnhubUrl, budgetPerMinute }, tw: { mis, yahootw, misUrl, misAck: { version } | null, yahootwAck: { version } | null, provider: "yahoo"|"mis" }, crypto: { binanceUrl } } }`.
- `data/wickline/secrets.json`: `{ "finnhub.key": "<key>" }`.
- `localStorage` `wickline.filter`: `all`, `us`, `tw`, `hk`, `cn`, `jp`, `kr`, `crypto` or `other`; each use in a `try`.

## Modules

Notes beyond each module's line in RECIPE.md's Built from:

- `main.js`: the `View` is `gpui-kit`'s `View`, with `gpui-base` `v_flex`, `h_flex`, `InputState`. The detail dialog opens with `openOverlay({ mod: "wickline", kind: "dialog", width: 440, props: { detail: quote } })` (`ui/detail-dialog.js`), the settings dialog as a modal one with `settings: { tab, health }` (`ui/settings-dialog.js`); draft, inputs and Save stay on the view.
- `ui/`: built from `gpui-kit` `div`, `svg`, `PathBuilder`, `Background`, `window.paint_path` and `gpui-base` `h_flex`, `v_flex`, `Input`, `Checkbox`, `Switch`. `rowFrame` holds the handle, `×`, hairline and drop line. `chart` paints each by `window.paint_path(...)` as an absolute layer over a sized `div`: the line (`PathBuilder.stroke`, 2px with the fill by default, else 1.25px; 1.5px in the sidebar and table), the rule (`.dash_array([3, 3])`), the fill (`PathBuilder.fill()`, closed along the bottom); `chartGeometry` (`format.js`) gives `[x%, y%]` points, 8% headroom, the previous close in range.
- `watchlist.js`: the host's `shown()` defaults to true and comes from `layout()` for a docked block. `main.js` runs each source's `runProvider(id, io)` in its own `cx.spawn`, with its `cx.sleep` and `cx.notify()`. Each source keeps a cooldown and a problem (`problems()`: status lines, the dialog's `health`) and quotes the symbols of its markets and kinds, within its `cap` (first in list order). An overlay source writes its own prices and errors (by symbol, then source), never the base quotes; the `line` a row merges is that source's same-day line or the base's. A source that is a symbol's sole source (Yahoo 奇摩股市 for a TW-only symbol or a future) gives that row its errors and periods; its price stays in the source's own slot. An index whose market resolves wakes each overlay source it joins, never a full restart. The Finnhub key, reread when `keyRev` changes, stays in memory and requests, never `config()`. Edits (`restore` undoes `remove`) are synchronous, bump a revision (a round in flight drops its late results) and wake every loop, as `sync()` does for another copy's edit. A source declares `id`, `markets`, `kinds`, `overlay`, `cap(config)`, `manualRefresh`, `spacingMs`, `intervalMs`, `linesMs`, `linesPerRound`, `requests` and `decode` (`providers/provider.js`); `Watchlist.serves` decides which symbols it gets, and a `QuoteError` with `stopsSource` stops its loop. Between rounds a loop also wakes at the shown rows' next edge (`edgeAt`, from `freshness.js`'s `nextEdge`; `{ openings: true }` for openings only), the moments a row's session or day can turn: a period's pre, regular and post bounds (else a Taiwan or US quote's exchange hours on a trading weekday; not FX), the exchange's lunch inside the regular period, a future's segments (its line's and TAIFEX's table, which alone holds the 15:00 night once `ownPeriods` drops the line's), FX's Friday and Sunday 22:00 UTC, and a Taiwan row's Taipei midnight. Openings (a regular start, a lunch's end, a segment's start, FX's Sunday 22:00) also end the interval and start a round; any other edge only redraws. A hidden copy skips edges until `wakeMissed()`.
- `secrets.js`: the `system` holds `platform()` (from `window().platform`) and `chmod(path)`, the one `exec` under Programs, run after each write on `macos` and `linux` only; the folder is `appHome()` + `/data/wickline`. The file goes with its last key; save and remove resolve false when chmod failed (the key is still written).
- `layout.js`: `TABLE_COLUMNS` (id, width, alignment); `tableColumns(width)` gives the ids that fit, in display order; `nameWidth`.
- `moves.js`: runs only `tab.new`, `tab.select`, `pane.split`, `pane.close`, `layout.hide`, `layout.show` and `layout.select`. Its `syncDock` (from `render`, for the view) and `onScreen` (for the loops) read the block's place, yield and shown state, both clearing a stale `wickline.dockTab`.
- `markets.js`: `marketOf` judges by form; the kinds are stock, index, pair, future (`WTX&`, Taiwan by form) and other. `exchangeRule(exchange, symbol)` gives `{ realtime, delayS, lunch, futures, fx }` (`.TW`/`.TWO` TAI's if unlisted, `=F`/`=X` without one); `TYPICAL_EXCHANGE` (us NMS, tw TAI). `TAIFEX_HOURS` is TAIFEX's own table (day 08:45–13:45, night 15:00–05:00, Taipei minutes, Monday to Friday openings), apart from Yahoo Finance's `RULES`: `freshness.js` reads a future's session from its line's segments, else from it (`futuresSegment`, `sessionName`), and `watchlist.js` cuts a wait at its next opening (`edgeAt`, from `freshness.js`'s `nextEdge`). `TWSE_HOURS` (09:00–13:30 Taipei, TWSE and TPEx) and `US_HOURS` (09:30–16:00 New York, its offset from `usOffsetMin`: daylight saving by hand, second Sunday in March to first Sunday in November) give a Taiwan or US quote its session only until Yahoo's periods arrive (`hoursWindow` in `freshness.js`); any other market stays unknown, and the view shows no word for it. `ownPeriods` drops a line's `segments` once `now` is on a later trading day than theirs, so from 15:00 the night is read from the table. The market filter is read per render. Yahoo's exchange codes (closed): US DJI, SNP, NIM, NMS, NYQ, PCX live, CXI 20, NYS, WCB 15; Taiwan TAI, TWO 20, TFE; HKG, SHH, SHZ, JPX, OSA 15; KSC, KOE 20; rule only: NGM, NCM, ASE, BTS, CCY (FX) live, PNK 15, CME, NYM, CMX, CBT futures 10, read through `exchangeRule(exchange, symbol)`.
- `calendar.js`: `taipeiDay`, `tradingDay`, `futuresDay`, `dayOf`, moved from `providers/yahootw.js`, which re-exports the first two; `closureOf` is the one rules table for a row's closure, listed and inferred, as one tagged value (`closure.kind`: `listed`, `noTrades`, `nightRemoved` or `nightNoTrades`), and `deadlineOf` gives its next inference deadline.
- `motion.js`: `rowsWithGhosts` keeps a removed row, from data copied before its quote goes, at its measured height and old place, unless the filter hides it; `settleOffset` starts a dropped row at `relative().top(from)`, its move in measured row heights. A native `transition` (`opacity`, `height`, `top`) animates only a later change, so `playRow` draws the start value, flips to the end 32 ms later (two `cx.sleep`s), then drops the state.
- `i18n.js`: the tables use `{name}` placeholders; English includes `quotes.js`'s error texts. The watchlist hands the view `{ code, params }` (a removal's adds `undo`); the view translates when it draws.
- `settings.js`: `readSettings` checks every field, defaults otherwise; `validateSettings` maps a field path to an error code, `{}` when valid; `mergeSettings` keeps unknown keys, also inside `sources`; for the consents an older statement version counts as none; in `currentHealth` a problem seen at opening holds while the settings its source reads are unchanged. Also `upIsRed` and the key's name, `finnhub.key`.
