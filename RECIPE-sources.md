# Wickline — sources

Each source's requests, the fields its decoder reads and its detailed rules, linked from [RECIPE.md](RECIPE.md) and [RECIPE-internals.md](RECIPE-internals.md); the stored layouts, modules and moves are in the latter. Hosts, programs and storage locations are named in RECIPE.md; nothing here changes them.

## Network requests

Each source's requests and the fields its decoder reads (headers, hosts and the person's URLs under RECIPE.md's Network):

- `https://query1.finance.yahoo.com/v8/finance/chart/<symbol>?interval=5m&range=1d&includePrePost=false`: `chart.result[0].meta` (`regularMarketPrice`, `regularMarketTime`, `exchangeName`, `previousClose` else `chartPreviousClose`, `currency`, `longName`/`shortName`, `regularMarketDayHigh`/`Low`, `regularMarketVolume` (none for an index or `CCC` crypto, in the quote currency; ÷1000 as lots for `.TW`/`.TWO`, else shares), `currentTradingPeriod.pre/regular/post`) and `indicators.quote[0]` (`close` for the line, the first `open`), with `timestamp` (s): each point at its bar's start (5 min bars), a missing close dropped with its time; no `timestamp`, no times. A response whose `meta.symbol` differs from the request is rejected.
- `https://data-api.binance.vision/api/v3/ticker/24hr?symbols=["BTCUSDT",...]`: `lastPrice`, `prevClosePrice`, `openPrice`, `highPrice`, `lowPrice`, `volume` (base asset; compact from 1,000,000: `1.0B DOGE`, `10.12 億 DOGE`), `closeTime`.
- `https://data-api.binance.vision/api/v3/klines?symbol=BTCUSDT&interval=15m&limit=96`: closes (index 4) for the 24-hour line, each at its bar's open time (index 0, ms).
- Finnhub (chosen, key saved): `https://finnhub.io/api/v1/quote?symbol=AAPL` (or the saved Finnhub URL with `/quote?symbol=`), header `X-Finnhub-Token: <key>`: `c`, `pc`, `o`, `h`, `l`, `t`.
- Yahoo 奇摩股市 (on, consented): `https://tw.stock.yahoo.com/_td-stock/api/resource/StockServices.stockList;symbols=2330.TW,6488.TWO`: rows matched on `symbol`: `symbolName`, `regularMarketTime` (ISO), `exchangeDataDelayedBy`, `exchange`, `currency`, and as `{ raw }` (`-` when none) `price`, `regularMarketPreviousClose`, `regularMarketOpen`, `regularMarketDayHigh`/`Low`, `limitUpPrice`/`limitDownPrice`, `volumeK` (thousands: lots; a future's contracts ×1000; none for an index), and plain `previousVolumeK`, `singleVolumeK` (last size), `turnoverM` (millions; 0 is none), `avgPrice` (`-` none), `inMarket`/`outMarket` (inner/outer) and `totalMarketVolK`, scaled alike; none for an index. Inner and outer are kept only when their sum equals `totalMarketVolK` exactly (totals by `amount`: a number or numeric string, 0 kept, `" "`/`-` none); else both dropped, the rest kept. `orderbook` (`bid`/`ask`, `bidVolK`/`askVolK`) with `sumBidVolK`/`sumAskVolK`: a side whose sizes as sent (unpriced sizeless: 0; unreadable priced: fails) differ from its total drops the book; `[]`, totals 0: empty; 5 shown a side, sizeless ones skipped, unpriced ones with a size market orders (`price` null). A `preopen` stock row, no price: its book as `auctionBook`. Lines: `…/FinanceChartService.ApacLibraCharts;type=tick;symbols=%5B%222330.TW%22%5D`: `[0].chart` with `meta.symbol`, `meta.currentTradingPeriod`, for a future `meta.sourceTradingPeriods` (its sessions, nested by day; kept flat and sorted as `periods.segments`), `timestamp` (s) and `indicators.quote[0].close`. The symbol's kind (`kindOf`) picks the feed clock and the day key (`tradingDay`): a future's (`WTX&`, rows from TFE only) or a stock's (stocks and indices, TAI and TWO together).
- MIS (on, consented): `https://mis.twse.com.tw/stock/api/getStockInfo.jsp?ex_ch=tse_2330.tw|otc_6488.tw&json=1&delay=0` (or the saved MIS URL with the same query): `msgArray[]` rows matched on `ex` and `c`, with `z` (last trade, `-` when none), `b`/`a` (best bids and asks, `_`-separated) with `g`/`f` (sizes, lots), `y`, `o`, `h`, `l`, `n`, `tlong` (ms), `u`/`w` (limit up and down), `v` (volume, lots; never read from a flagged row), `tv` (last size, lots, a traded row only), `ts` (`1` while the row is in a call auction) and `pz` (that auction's trial price).
- TWSE holiday schedule (while the list holds a Taiwan symbol, once per Taipei date): `https://openapi.twse.com.tw/v1/holidaySchedule/holidaySchedule`, a JSON array whose rows give `Name` (the closure's reason, trimmed) and `Date` (ROC, `1151225`: year 115 + 1911, month, day); `Weekday` and `Description` are not read. A row whose `Name` holds 交易日, 開始交易 or 最後交易 is a notice of a trading day (國曆新年開始交易日), not a closure (loose on purpose: a missed closure is still inferred); a Saturday or Sunday date is dropped; any other name is a closed weekday (市場無交易，僅辦理結算交割作業 included). Only the first fetch of a Taipei date holds a Taiwan round, by the 10 s timeout at most; a failure (also a list without a closed weekday of the current Taipei year, such as an empty one) keeps the held list and is retried 1 h after it started, beside the rounds, and never reaches the status line. Kept in memory only. A listed date reads `holiday` for stocks and indices from 00:00 Taipei; for futures from 05:00 when the weekday before ran a night session into it, else from 00:00. From 00:00 to 05:00 after a listed weekday, the night it removed reads closed (`日盤 08:45–13:45 · 夜盤休市`). A trade at or after the current session's open in any row of a market (stocks and indices: 09:00; futures: 08:45, 15:00, or the day before's 15:00 until 05:00) overrides both for every row of that market. Licence: Open Government Data License, version 1.0; its attribution statement: 「臺灣證券交易所 有價證券集中交易市場開（休）市日期（每年最新版）；此開放資料依政府資料開放授權條款 (Open Government Data License) 進行公眾釋出，使用者於遵守本條款各項規定之前提下，得利用之。https://data.gov.tw/license」
- Inferred closures (`No trades today`, `今日無成交`; never 休市, which only the schedule gives): per Taiwan market and session (stocks and indices: 09:00; futures: 08:45, and 15:00 until 05:00), a copy keeps in memory the newest trade any answer carried (whatever its day; the session has traded once it is at or after the open) and each source's latest answer: when it arrived and whether the source is real time. Yahoo 奇摩股市's stock list reports `newestTrade` per market clock: the newest `regularMarketTime` among the requested rows with a positive price, a trade time the decoder accepts (a future's at most 5 min ahead, a stock's not after today) and `exchangeDataDelayedBy` 0 (futures only from TFE), so a delayed or `-` row is neither an answer nor a trade; MIS each quote's `tlong` when `z` priced it (a book price, or a row flagged in a call auction, is a snapshot, no trade, so a closed, `休市` or `今日無成交` label over it has no time, only an earlier day's date), Yahoo Finance each Taiwan quote's time (real time by its exchange). Only sources that are on count, listed real time first, each kind in `PROVIDERS` order (Yahoo 奇摩股市, MIS, Yahoo Finance): turning one off drops its reading and note at once. A session reads no trades from its open + 10 min with a real-time answer, or + 30 min with a Yahoo Finance one (20 min late), each only when that kind's latest answer arrived at or after the deadline − 2 min, so one answer at the open and then an outage never counts; the first counting answer names the source on the status line, so the note never alternates. The deadlines are row edges (`closureOf` gives the next one beside the closure, carried as `closureDeadline`): a redraw, not a round. Veto: a futures trade since 08:45 (the night answers' newest included, for a copy shown after 15:00) blocks a stock inference; a stock trade since 09:00 blocks the futures day session's. The night: after a day that traded (its day session, the night answers' newest trade at or after 08:45, or stocks since 09:00), the closure `nightNoTrades`: `已收盤 · 13:45`, `日盤 08:45–13:45 · 夜盤尚無成交`, and `<source> 夜盤尚無成交。`; after a day without trades, `今日無成交` again. On such a day the day session's reading carries from 15:00 to the night's deadline (15:10, a row edge also when no night answer comes), and from 00:00 to 05:00 the night's reading of the day before holds (`今日無成交` until 05:00), unless the new date is listed (休市). A listed day comes first; a trade since the open clears everything at once; a weekend never reads it.

**Yahoo 奇摩股市** (off by default): the same overlay for Taiwan stocks and indices (symbols URL-encoded), from the fixed `tw.stock.yahoo.com`, with its own switch and consent, whose statement names what is asked by exchange (TWSE, TPEx, TAIFEX) (`sources.tw.yahootw`, `yahootwAck.version` = `YAHOOTW_CONSENT_VERSION`, 2; a MIS consent never counts). A row is priced only with a positive price, its trade on today's trading day in Taipei (UTC+8 by hand) and `exchangeDataDelayedBy` 0; a `-` row gives only its name (before the open, a TW-only index's also its previous close: below). A future's row counts only from `exchange` TFE (any other gives nothing, its name ignored, so it reads not found) and is never discarded for its limits (still kept for lighting). A price outside its own `limitDownPrice`..`limitUpPrice` is discarded with a reason, and so is a priced row of a symbol priced here alone (below) that is delayed, has no trade time or is over 5 min ahead (`Yahoo TW Stock returned no real-time prices.`); any other rejected row, or a symbol missing from the answer, gives nothing; while open, an answer with prices but none accepted (not `-` only) says on the status line it returned no real-time prices, until one is priced. A future (`^[A-Z]{1,6}[&@]$`, such as `WTX&`; market Taiwan by its form) is TW-only by form: priced here alone, never sent to Yahoo Finance, MIS or Finnhub; with this source off it is one unavailable line, `Futures quotes need Yahoo TW Stock turned on in Settings.`, or the re-confirm reason while its consent awaits re-confirmation (a TW-only index too, in place of not found), gone as soon as the source is on; a code it does not answer reads `Ticker not found on Yahoo TW Stock.`. No other unknown symbol is sent.

Yahoo 奇摩股市, past the request above: A future's row counts only while at most 5 min ahead of the local clock, on any day (also a later day's: a clock behind Yahoo's at 15:00); a future's trading day starts at 15:00 in Taipei and is the next weekday's (Friday night is Monday's), holidays unknown. Every row priced today is timed at the newest of today's trades on its own market clock: futures (TFE) on theirs, stocks and indices (TAI and TWO together, whatever `exchange` says) on the stock clock, so a quiet stock never takes a night futures trade (`tradeTime` keeps its own). Its line (the trading day's one-minute closes, a future's from 15:00 the day before, so the night's minutes stay past midnight; a quiet minute carrying the last close, leading empty minutes and any after now (a future's answer lists the whole day) dropped; each point at its own minute, a carried one too; `meta.symbol` must match) replaces Yahoo's only while its price is shown and of the line's day, ending at that price. A failed, empty or frozen line (on arrival, its last minute with a close over 2 min before the newest trade seen at this or an earlier arrival whose minute ended over 2 min ago, as a line holds a trade only once its minute ends, that trade capped at the end of the line's regular session (the answer's `currentTradingPeriod`, else the symbol's known periods), so a stock's 14:30 fixed-price trade never counts; so with lines 2 min apart a frozen line goes 4 min after its first arrival, 485-595 s after it stops mid-session, at once if first asked after the close over 3 min after the last trade, and stays gone, also through a dropped price), or losing the price, drops the line; a source failure also shows on the status line until that symbol's next line arrives. A `^TW…` index Yahoo Finance lacks is priced here alone while on (TW-only, in memory): market from its `exchange`, session from its line's `currentTradingPeriod`, an earlier day's row as its closed quote at its own time, not found until priced or named. Before the open a `preopen` row with no price (from about 08:30) gives such an index its `regularMarketPreviousClose` as its closed quote, timed at 13:30 on the day of the newest earlier-day trade among the copy's Taiwan quotes (Yahoo Finance's), else on TWSE's last trading day before today by the calendar (the last weekday the holiday schedule does not list), dated again with each such answer, and without a change, so it reads `已收盤 · <date> 13:30` as `^TWII` does; never for a future (its rows keep their last trade), never over a price already held from that close or later; with no other Taiwan quote the calendar can name a day TWSE did not trade (after a closure the schedule does not list, such as a typhoon day, without the schedule, or on the first trading day of a year, as the schedule holds only the current one); it is no price from this source, so such an answer still counts as no answer, no trade and no missing real-time price. Once priced, a TW-only row keeps its last price through a failed round or a rejected or missing row (shown cached, with the reason) and through a name-only answer (unmarked).

TWSE MIS, past the request above: The price is the last trade (`z`); with none in the last 5-second snapshot, the midpoint of the best bid and ask, skipping `0.0000` levels. One side only: a stock locked at its limit is priced at it (asks only, best at limit-down `w`; bids only, best at limit-up `u`; both limits present); any other one-sided row has no price, no error. Limits unchecked without both, or at `u` 9999.95 (MIS's none). A price outside `w`..`u` from the same answer is discarded with a reason in the detail, no status line. A row flagged in a call auction (`ts` `1` with a snapshot time, whatever `pz` reads: from 08:30 to the open, from 13:25 to the close) is neither a price nor an error, whatever its book: the MIS price held from before it stays, else the row shows Yahoo's quote. A copy keeps the flagged snapshot's `tlong` per symbol in memory (`auctionAt`), unless the held MIS price is newer; a MIS answer that prices the symbol or no longer flags it, a failed MIS request and MIS turned off drop it. While the flag is under 2 min old (`auctionFlagged`), the held MIS price never outranks a Yahoo 奇摩股市 price of the same Taipei day, and a row whose shown price traded before the snapshot reads `auction` (`Watchlist#marked` passes `auctionAt` then). A Yahoo 奇摩股市 trade at or after the snapshot ends it. A names-only row (before 08:30: no `ts`, no `pz`) is no flag. Levels: 5 a side, `-` prices, nonpositive sizes skipped, `0.0000` with a size a market order (`price` null): a priced row's `book`; a flagged row's `auctionBook`, kept as `auctionBooks` with `auctionAt` by the newest flag; a named row neither flagged nor priced without one: `noLevels` (an empty book) until MIS answers otherwise, fails or is off. Only symbols asked for are accepted. Codes keep case (`tse_00632R.tw`; lower case gets an empty answer). `provider` (0.3.1's key) is written from `mis` on every save and wins on load (a downgrade keeps the choice).

## Source notes

Per-source request, field and constant notes.

- **Yahoo TW Stock**: price limit fields: `limitUpPrice`/`limitDownPrice`, read by `priceLimit`. Lines: After its prices, Yahoo 奇摩股市 asks lines, one symbol each, only for those just priced: each at most every 2 min (also in the closing auction; 10 when not open; after the close, never once it has that day's line), at most 3 per round, longest-waiting first. Rate limit: Yahoo 奇摩股市 also 403 (a 429 on every source).
- **MIS**: price limit fields: `u`/`w`, read by `priceLimit`. Consent: MIS runs only while `sources.tw.mis` is true and `misAck.version` equals `MIS_CONSENT_VERSION` (2). Raise `MIS_CONSENT_VERSION` or `YAHOOTW_CONSENT_VERSION` with any change in substance to that source's zh-TW statement or consent (the zh-TW text prevails).
- **Binance**: each pair's 24-hour line at most every 60 s (at once for a new pair or URL). A failed batch is retried pair by pair; a failed line keeps the last one. Binance HTTP 451 is the region error. Rate limit: Binance also 418.
- **Finnhub**: Finnhub overlays price (`c`), previous close (`pc`), open, high, low and time (`t`, s). A `c` or `t` of 0 (unknown symbol) is no price, no error. `BRK-B` is asked as `BRK.B`. It runs only while `sources.us.provider` is `finnhub` and this copy has read a key. Only the first `floor(budgetPerMinute / 4)` US stocks in list order use it. Requests: spaced `max(1 s, interval / symbols)` across rounds too.
